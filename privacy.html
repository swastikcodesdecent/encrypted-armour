Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

At Encrypted Armour, privacy is not a feature — it is the foundation. This policy explains exactly what data we collect, how we use it, and the architectural guarantees that protect your organization's information.

Effective: January 1, 2025
Updated: May 10, 2025
Applies globally
01

Overview

Encrypted Armour ("we," "our," or "us") operates a highly secure enterprise workspace platform designed for organizations that require confidential internal collaboration. This Privacy Policy governs the collection, use, processing, and protection of personal data when you access or use our platform, website, APIs, and associated services (collectively, the "Services").

This policy applies to all users of the Encrypted Armour platform including administrators, employees of client organizations, and visitors to our website. By using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

Our core commitment: Encrypted Armour operates on a zero-knowledge architecture. We cannot read the content of your encrypted messages, files, or communications. Your organization controls the encryption keys, not us.

02

Information We Collect

We collect the minimum information necessary to provide, maintain, and improve the Services. We categorize this data as follows:

Category Examples Purpose
Account Information Name, work email, organization name, role Authentication and account management
Usage Metadata Login timestamps, session duration, feature interactions Security monitoring and product improvement
Device & Technical Data IP address, browser type, OS version, device identifiers Fraud prevention and security enforcement
Billing Information Payment method type, billing address, invoice history Processing subscriptions (via PCI-DSS compliant processors)
Support Communications Support tickets, chat logs with our team Customer support and issue resolution
Workspace Content Messages, files, tasks (encrypted, inaccessible to us) Delivered to users — we cannot access the plaintext

What we do NOT collect: We do not collect the content of end-to-end encrypted messages, file contents, conference call audio/video, or any workspace data in plaintext form. This content is encrypted client-side before transmission.

03

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect strictly for the following purposes:

  • Service delivery: Providing, operating, and maintaining the Encrypted Armour platform and all associated features.
  • Security and fraud prevention: Detecting unauthorized access, monitoring for anomalous behavior, and enforcing Zero Trust policies across sessions.
  • Authentication: Verifying user identity through multi-factor authentication, SSO integrations, and session management.
  • Billing and account management: Processing payments, managing subscriptions, and issuing invoices to enterprise clients.
  • Product improvement: Analyzing aggregated, anonymized usage patterns to improve performance, reliability, and the user experience.
  • Legal compliance: Meeting obligations under applicable data protection laws including GDPR, CCPA, and regional equivalents.
  • Customer support: Responding to support requests, resolving technical issues, and communicating service updates.

We do not use your data for advertising, do not sell it to third parties, and do not use it to train AI or machine learning models without explicit consent.

04

Data Sharing

Encrypted Armour does not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

Recipient Reason Safeguards
Infrastructure providers Cloud hosting and storage operations DPA agreements, encrypted-at-rest data only
Payment processors Billing and subscription management PCI-DSS Level 1 certified partners
Security vendors Threat detection and DDoS protection Minimal metadata only, strict DPA
Legal authorities Valid legal orders (warrants, court orders) We notify users where legally permitted and challenge overbroad requests
Your organization's admins Workspace administration and compliance Controlled by your organization's own policies

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user data may be transferred. We will provide notice before such a transfer takes place and ensure the acquiring entity maintains equivalent privacy protections.

05

Data Storage & Security

All data processed by Encrypted Armour is stored in SOC 2 Type II certified data centers with physical and logical access controls. We implement layered security measures throughout our infrastructure:

  • All data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.3 or higher.
  • Access to production systems is restricted to authorized personnel via hardware security keys and privileged access management (PAM) systems.
  • We conduct regular third-party penetration tests and vulnerability assessments.
  • Automated anomaly detection monitors for unauthorized access attempts 24/7.
  • Full audit logs are maintained for all administrative actions with immutable, tamper-proof storage.
  • Our infrastructure undergoes continuous compliance monitoring against CIS benchmarks.

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected users and relevant supervisory authorities within 72 hours of discovery, as required by GDPR and applicable regulations.

06

Encryption Architecture

Encrypted Armour is built on a zero-knowledge encryption model. This section provides technical transparency about how your data is protected:

Zero-knowledge guarantee: All workspace content — messages, files, conference calls, task data — is encrypted on your device before being transmitted to our servers. Encryption keys are derived from your credentials and never leave your device in plaintext form. This means Encrypted Armour employees cannot access the content of your workspace, under any circumstances.

  • End-to-end encryption (E2EE): Applied to all messages, file transfers, and conference calls using the Signal Protocol and AES-256-GCM.
  • Ephemeral session keys: Conference calls use unique per-session keys that are discarded after the call ends — no recordings are retained on our servers unless explicitly enabled by your admin.
  • Forward secrecy: Compromise of long-term keys does not compromise past session keys, ensuring historical communications remain protected.
  • Key management: Organization administrators control key rotation policies and can revoke access for departing employees without requiring re-encryption of all data.
  • Zero Trust enforcement: Every API request is authenticated and authorized independently — no implicit trust is granted based on network location.
07

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have specific rights over your personal data. We honor these rights for all users globally, regardless of whether local law strictly requires it:

Right to Access

Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you in a portable, machine-readable format.

Right to Rectification

Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information associated with your account.

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten") subject to legal retention obligations.

Right to Object

Object to specific processing activities, including any analytics or profiling operations.

Right to Restriction

Request that we restrict processing of your data while a dispute or review is in progress.

Right to Portability

Receive your data in a structured, interoperable format to transfer to another service provider.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@encryptedarmour.io. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

08

Cookies & Tracking

We use a minimal set of cookies to operate the Services securely and effectively. We do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking.

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Strictly Necessary Session management, authentication tokens, CSRF protection Session / 24 hours
Security Fraud detection, bot prevention, Zero Trust verification signals Up to 30 days
Preferences UI settings, theme, language, notification preferences Up to 1 year
Analytics (optional) Aggregated, anonymized product usage — no personal identifiers Up to 90 days

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings or via the cookie preference center within the platform. Disabling strictly necessary cookies will impair platform functionality. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels under any circumstances.

09

Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to provide the Services and meet legal obligations. Our retention schedule is as follows:

  • Account data: Retained for the duration of your subscription and deleted within 30 days of account termination upon written request.
  • Workspace content (encrypted): Controlled entirely by your organization. Admins may delete content at any time. Upon account termination, all encrypted blobs are deleted within 30 days.
  • Security logs: Retained for 12 months for incident investigation purposes, then purged.
  • Billing records: Retained for 7 years as required by financial regulations.
  • Support records: Retained for 2 years from ticket closure to facilitate follow-up issues.
  • Conference recordings (if enabled by admin): Retained per your organization's configured policy, defaulting to 90 days.

After deletion, data is overwritten using DoD 5220.22-M compliant multi-pass erasure on physical storage. Cloud storage volumes are cryptographically erased prior to decommissioning.

10

Children's Privacy

Encrypted Armour is an enterprise platform intended exclusively for use by adults in a professional context. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under the age of 16.

If you believe a minor has accessed the platform without authorization, please contact us immediately at privacy@encryptedarmour.io and we will investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion of any data associated with that account.

11

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our Services, legal requirements, or data practices. When we make material changes, we will:

  • Post the updated policy on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
  • Notify all active account holders via email at least 14 days before the changes take effect.
  • Display an in-platform banner for 30 days following significant changes.
  • Maintain a public changelog of all revisions to this policy for transparency.

Continued use of the Services after the effective date of an updated policy constitutes acceptance of the changes. If you do not agree with the updated policy, you may terminate your account before the effective date.

12

Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal data, please reach out through any of the following channels:

Data Protection Officer

privacy@encryptedarmour.io  ·  Response within 2 business days

Contact DPO

For legal notices and formal requests, you may also write to us at:
