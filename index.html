Platform Features

Built for the modern enterprise

Every feature engineered for security, speed, and seamless collaboration across your entire organization.

End-to-End Encryption

AES-256 encryption ensures every message, file, and workflow remains private. Zero-knowledge architecture means only your team holds the keys.

Team Collaboration

Real-time collaboration across departments with secure channels, thread-based discussions, and smart notification controls — built for scale.

Workspace Management

Organize teams, projects, and workflows inside structured secure workspaces. Full admin controls with granular permission management.

Encrypted Messaging

Direct messages, group chats, and broadcast channels all secured with end-to-end encryption. No metadata leakage. No backdoors.

Internal Task Coordination

Assign, track, and complete tasks across teams with full audit trails. Encrypted task boards with deadline management and priority queues.

Secure File Sharing

Share documents, assets, and data internally with controlled access. Files are encrypted at rest and in transit with expiring link support.

Introducing Now

Secure Online Conferencing

Host fully encrypted video and audio meetings inside your protected workspace — no third-party servers, no data leakage. Every call is shielded with end-to-end encryption, ephemeral session keys, and Zero Trust participant verification, so your boardroom stays private no matter where your team is.
